Hi, I'm Mohamed. This is the first post on a site I've been slowly putting together, so it feels right to start by saying hello properly.

## What I do

I'm a software engineer, mostly on the backend and infrastructure side. The part of a product most people never see is the part I like best: the pipelines, services and plumbing that quietly have to work every single day. These days I work remotely, focusing on international exposure and contributing to Datastory, a very exciting data visualization platform launched from Stockholm to the world.

## How I got here

Since I was very little, I was always curious about how things work. I disassembled toys, my first computer, and other things to try to find and fix the problems. I eventually lucked out by studying Computer Engineering in college and got my bachelor's degree from the German University in Cairo, where I got to play with computers, create interesting projects and make good friends.

I started my career during my last year of studies, building mobile apps for a travel technology startup, where I got to see how applications are developed, tested and shipped. I also did some interesting research on the emerging cross-platform mobile frameworks of the time. After that I developed a passion for backend engineering and moved to a backend position in the same company.

The next chapter was joining a promising small startup in its very early stages, working with data and social media analytics. It was a rare opportunity to lay the foundation for the web and the backend. I got my first taste of shipping to production and the commitments that follow, and thankfully I watched its product grow from four users to a few hundred, which taught me more about teams and hiring than any book.

Then came a couple of years at Dell, in its core function, as a software engineer in the 5G team, where the systems were big, serious and wonderfully unforgiving. Now I'm working remotely for Datastory, a startup in Stockholm developing a platform for AI-powered data storytelling and visualization.

## Other side quests

Growing up, my family, especially my father, believed that sports were non-negotiable for developing character. I played along and joined the swimming team at a very young age. I loved it, I was good at it, and I spent more than four hours a day in swim training. I was lucky to meet my lifelong friends on that team; we had a very special bond and shared almost everything.

Music was a language we spoke as well, sharing songs and albums from a very young age to this day. We took it a step further and decided to teach ourselves some instruments and play together, until it became a passion and a little playground for some healthy competition. I studied music theory for fun, still play a little guitar and keyboard, and hold those memories close to my chest.

## Why this place exists

In my journey I learned the value of being expressive, getting myself out into the world and sharing my experience. Like many others, I experienced some isolation after 2020, and I think the best way to avoid feeling that way is to actively reach out, express and engage. I decided to create this space to announce my little achievements, share lessons learned and be a little window into who I am.
